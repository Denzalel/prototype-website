Support and wellbeing

How UniSuper helps support members affected by domestic and family violence

Finding support can be an important first step. Explore services, workplace information and ways we can help you feel safer when managing your super.

‹ UniSuper  ›  Support and wellbeing

Support when you need it

Everyone’s circumstances are different. If domestic or family violence is affecting you, there are specialist services and practical steps that may help. You can also ask us about extra security when you contact UniSuper.

Specialist health and wellbeing support

Did you know UniSuper’s insurer, MetLife, offers 360Health? It can connect eligible members with specialist mental health support, coaching and other services.

About 360Health at UniSuper ↗Visit MetLife 360Health ↗

Paid family and domestic violence leave

All employees, including casual employees, are entitled to 10 days of paid family and domestic violence leave. Learn about the leave and how to access it.

Extra security for your account

When speaking with our staff by phone or in person, you can request extra security measures. These can help protect your account from potential security risks and unwanted access.

Contact UniSuper for support

Get help and make a safety plan

Specialist services can help you understand your options, plan for safety and connect with local support. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.

1800RESPECT — 24/7 support ↗

Let us know how we can support you

You can tell us what would help you feel safer when dealing with your super. Our team can discuss extra account security and communication preferences.

Other information that may be useful

These independent services can provide confidential information, counselling and practical assistance.

1800RESPECT

National domestic, family and sexual violence counselling and support, available day and night.

Need urgent help?

If someone is in immediate danger, call 000. For confidential counselling and support in Australia, call 1800 737 732 or visit 1800RESPECT.
